# Individual contribution statement — B

> AIAA 4051 Natural Language Processing, Fall 2026.
> Conformal Prediction for Reliable Short- and Long-Form Question Answering.

I was responsible for the Conformal Factuality long-form question-answering
component. I reproduced the released pipeline's calibration, claim filtering,
and evaluation stages on the cached FActScore data, with fixed disjoint
calibration and test splits. I produced the two-target, three-calibration-size
result grid, response-level bootstrap intervals, and provenance-stamped result
files. I also clarified that the conformal guarantee counts empty responses as
vacuously correct, while reporting the separate nonempty factuality and
retention costs needed to judge whether an answer remains useful.

I analyzed all 210 claims in the fixed test split to explain why frequency
filtering has high retained-claim precision but low recall: score boundaries,
unrelated rather than contradictory support, compound and referential claims,
subjective statements, ambiguous entities, and repeated errors all contribute.
I documented successful, incomplete, empty, and failed cases; wrote Sections
1b, 2b, and 3b; created the retention–factuality and claim-diagnostic figures;
and prepared the H3, H5, and H7 handoffs. For H3, I implemented the offline
claim-splitting, three-way judging, frequency aggregation, strict parsers, and
fake-client tests used when the extension moved to newly generated responses.

I am also responsible for assembling the team's final project poster. That work
will integrate the short-form, long-form, and extension results, the shared
tables and figures, the main limitations, and the individual contributions into
one coherent visual narrative. The poster itself is still in progress and is not
claimed as completed in this statement.
